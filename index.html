The Artist

Mudbone is a Nashville-based singer, songwriter, guitarist, and producer whose debut double album Rivers & Roads broke into the Top 100 on the Americana charts independently — twice. Gifted his blues name in a chance encounter with a protégé of blues pioneer Son House, he has spent 25 years and 250+ shows a year building one of the most versatile live acts in American roots music.

His performances scale from intimate solo acoustic sets to full 9-piece band productions, drawing fans of all ages and appealing across a vast demographic — exactly what casino and festival buyers need. He is a Van Go Records / Americana Music Association recording artist, holds endorsements with Taylor Guitars, Mesa Amps, Jim Dunlop, and MXR, and his new album arrives in 2026–2027.

Built for Every Stage

Full Band Production

Up to 9-piece configuration. Full rhythm section, horns, keys. High-energy roots rock set designed to move a crowd in any size room — from casino showroom to main stage festival.

Solo Acoustic

Stripped-down and intimate. Perfect for VIP lounges, private events, smaller casino rooms, or acoustic stages. The raw Delta blues tradition in its purest form.

Trio / Small Band

Flexible mid-scale option. All the energy of the full production in a compact, cost-effective package — ideal for festivals with production constraints or mid-size venues.
